**Search Console signals:**

- `minimalist whatsapp` — 2 clicks / 4 impressions = **50% CTR**. Tiny volume, perfect intent match. Strongest under-served signal we have.
- `how to make my whatsapp look like iphone` — 1/3. **"iPhone", not "iMessage"** — a distinct phrasing we don't target.
- iMessage page — 23 clicks / 551 impressions = 4.2% CTR over 3 months, our best-converting page.

- `whatsapp web refresh ctrl+r` cluster — ~186 impressions in 24h, **0 clicks**. Troubleshooting intent. **Deliberately excluded.**

**Extension feature usage (what people actually do):** Themes 21.6% → Visibility/Minimal Mode 20% → Fonts 17.4% → **Privacy blur 15.5%** → Backgrounds 12.4%.

**The gap this plan attacks:** Minimal Mode is our **#2 feature** and has one post. Privacy blur is our **#4 feature** and has **zero dedicated posts**. Both are core product, both are uncovered, and the one Minimal Mode post we have converts at 20% with a 50% query CTR. That's where the plan is weighted.

## Dependencies & caveats

- **Posts 2, 6, 8, 12, 14 need theme JSON files** (~13 total). Same blocker as the existing Tier 1 backlog.
- **Fix the blog `install_click` instrumentation first.** Until that ships, we cannot measure whether any of these 14 posts converts. We'd be flying blind on the exact question this plan is designed to answer.
- **Post 2 (iPhone) is the cannibalisation risk to watch.** If it can't be made genuinely distinct from the iMessage post in draft, retitle the iMessage post instead.
- **Sample-size honesty:** the conversion rates driving these choices come off 5–17 visitors per page. The *direction* is unambiguous (0 clicks vs 14 clicks isn't noise). The exact percentages will move.

## The one disagreement worth recording

Two posts a day on a domain this young still dilutes crawl budget and risks slowing the pages already ranking. Nine of these fourteen can ship immediately; **writing all fourteen and publishing them staggered would likely outperform publishing fourteen in seven days.** Your call — this plan follows the two-a-day decision as instructed.
